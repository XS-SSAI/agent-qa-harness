# Data attribution

Files `vegan_*.json` and `wedding_*.json` are filtered rows from the Overture Maps Foundation places theme, release 2026-10-21.0
(https://overturemaps.org). Most rows are CDLA-Permissive-2.0; rows sourced from Foursquare are Apache-2.0.
Each row keeps its `sources` field with dataset and licence. Data label: **public**.
`reference_*_top10.json` and `reference_wedding_*_top10.json` are derived by `tools/make_reference.py` (deterministic, no model).
