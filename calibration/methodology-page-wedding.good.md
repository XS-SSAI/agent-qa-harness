# How we verify listings

By Editorial Team

This site is an independent editorial directory for US couples and planners choosing wedding venues and vendors. Our publication standard is to check local facts against each business’s own website before a listing goes live.

Candidate listings come from the Overture Maps Foundation’s places data. Our candidate files contain filtered records from release 2026-10-21.0. These records provide a starting point for editorial review. Inclusion in the candidate data alone does not establish that a business belongs in our wedding directory.

Most records in our candidate files use the Community Data License Agreement – Permissive, Version 2.0 (CDLA-Permissive-2.0). Records sourced from Foursquare use the Apache License 2.0. Each candidate record retains its source and licence information.

Before publication, the Editorial Team must check the business’s own site to establish its local relevance and wedding offering. For a potential venue, this includes confirming that the specific place actually hosts weddings. An event-venue category or a familiar hotel name is not enough to establish that.

Candidates with doubtful wedding-venue status must be flagged for further review. Examples include a hotel chain entry, a tattoo studio, a brewery whose name indicates closure, a board-game cafe, or a community resource centre categorised as an event venue. These are reasons to investigate the candidate’s suitability before publication.

Hours and package prices require separate checks because they are not included in our candidate data. Before publishing either, the Editorial Team must check the business’s own website. Any hours, packages or prices included in a listing must be supported by that source. We do not fill missing details with estimates.

Verification must be dated. City and category pages may include only verified local listings drawn from the supplied candidate data. Nothing goes live without the directory owner’s approval. These requirements apply before publication, including when a candidate’s name or category initially appears relevant.

Featured listings and display advertising are planned ways to support the directory; they are not currently live. When introduced, featured placements will be clearly labelled so readers can identify paid visibility. Display ads will also be clearly distinguishable from editorial content.

Our editorial method is independent, and we do not sell positions in pay-to-rank rankings. A featured placement must be disclosed wherever it appears, so couples and planners can understand the commercial context while reviewing their options.
