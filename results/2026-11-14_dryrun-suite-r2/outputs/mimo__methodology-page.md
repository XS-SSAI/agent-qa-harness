# How we verify listings

## Where candidate listings come from

We do not scrape review sites or accept paid submissions as our starting point. Our candidate list comes from the Overture Maps Foundation places theme — an open map dataset published by a non-profit foundation. We work from release 2026-10-21.0, filtered to the venues relevant to this directory.

## What licence that data uses

Most rows in our files are released under CDLA-Permissive-2.0. Rows sourced from Foursquare carry Apache-2.0 instead. Each row keeps its own `sources` field, so the dataset and licence for that specific row stay attached to it. You can read the full breakdown in our data attribution notes.

The licence lets us use the data. It does not make the data true for your purposes. A place can appear in the dataset and still be closed, mis-categorised, or wrong about its own menu.

## What we check before a listing is published

Every candidate is checked against the business's own website before it goes live. Specifically:

- **Vegan status.** We look for the business's own statement about its menu — not a third-party tag, not a guess from the restaurant's name.
- **Menu proof.** We confirm the menu the business itself publishes, so the listing reflects what it actually serves.
- **Hours.** We take hours from the business's own site rather than from the map data.
- **Date.** Each check is dated, so you can see when the facts were last confirmed.

If the business's own site does not confirm something, we leave it out of the listing. Unknown stays unknown.

Nothing goes live without the owner's yes. If a business asks us to change or remove a listing, we act on it.

## How featured placements are disclosed

Featured placements are clearly labelled wherever they appear. A paid placement never reads as an editorial recommendation, and it never changes what we have verified about a business. Our editorial judgements are made independently of any commercial relationship.

## What we do not do

We do not invent listings, dishes, hours, prices, ratings, awards or reviews. If the data does not say it, and the business's own site does not confirm it, it is not on the page.
