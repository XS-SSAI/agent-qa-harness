# How we verify listings

We publish an independent editorial directory for US diners choosing vegan and vegan-friendly restaurants, cafes, bakeries and stores. Our aim is simple: give readers facts they can use now, not stale claims. Every listing follows the same path from candidate data to a published page.

## Where candidate listings come from

Candidate listings come from Overture Maps Foundation places data. Our working files are filtered rows from the Overture Maps places theme, release 2026-10-21.0. Most rows use the CDLA-Permissive-2.0 licence; rows sourced from Foursquare use Apache-2.0. Each row keeps its `sources` field with the dataset and licence. The data label is public.

A candidate row is a starting point, not a published listing. It tells us a place may exist and gives us a lead to check. It does not by itself prove vegan status, hours or menu.

## What we check before publishing

The editorial team checks each business's own site before a listing is published. We confirm vegan status, hours and menu details on that site, then date the check. That date becomes the listing's last-checked date. If the business's own site does not confirm vegan status, hours or menu details, we leave those details out.

We do not invent a listing, dish, hours, price, rating, award or review. We also do not treat the Overture row as proof of the facts above. Nothing goes live without the owner's yes. City pages list only verified listings from the supplied data.

## How featured placements are disclosed

Featured placements are planned, not live. When they are live, they will be clearly labeled as featured and disclosed on the page. The label makes the featured status clear to readers. Display ads and delivery affiliate links are also planned, not live. Display ads will never look like editorial content.

## What readers can expect

Published listings show the last-checked date for the facts we verified. We focus on the facts diners need to choose a place now: vegan status, menu proof, hours and last-checked date. We date our checks so readers can see how recent our verification is. We keep the method independent and the labels clear.
