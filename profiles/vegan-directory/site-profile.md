# Site profile: example vegan restaurant directory (US)

Real-world profile of a live niche directory project, with owner and company details removed. Newer rulings beat older ones. Unknown facts stay unknown. Invent nothing.

## What it sells
An independent editorial directory helping US diners choose vegan and vegan-friendly restaurants, cafes, bakeries and stores right now. Byline: "Editorial Team". Monetization (planned, not live): clearly labeled featured listings, delivery affiliate links, display ads that never look like editorial.

## Buyer
US diners choosing a vegan or vegan-friendly place to eat now (near me, city, cuisine, diet attributes).

## Pains and wants
Verified decision facts: vegan status, menu proof, hours, price, last-checked date, not stale user reviews. Filters such as open now, delivery or takeout, cuisine, diet, neighborhood. One intent per page.

## Rules
- Anonymous editorial-team byline. No named person, no personal story, no Person schema.
- Independent method; featured placements are always disclosed.
- One intent per page; no doorway pages. City pages list only verified listings from the supplied data.
- Never invent a listing, a dish, hours, prices, ratings, awards or reviews. If the data does not say it, leave it out.
- The data source is Overture Maps places (candidate list). Vegan status, hours and menus must still be checked on each business's own site before publishing, then dated.
- Nothing goes live without the owner's yes.

## Voice
Plain, factual, helpful. Short paragraphs. No hype.

## Banned phrases
- best in town
- world-famous
- mouth-watering
- hidden gem
- we personally tried
- our founder
