# Site profile: US wedding-vendor directory

Real-world profile of a live niche directory project, with owner and company details removed. Newer rulings beat older ones. Unknown facts stay unknown. Invent nothing.

## What it sells
An independent editorial directory helping US couples and planners choose wedding venues and vendors (venues, photographers, DJs, officiants, planners, caterers, and similar) right now. Byline: "Editorial Team". Monetization (planned, not live): clearly labeled featured listings and display ads that never look like editorial. No pay-to-rank rankings.

## Buyer
US couples and planners choosing wedding venues and vendors now (near me, city, style, budget).

## Pains and wants
A venue or vendor that fits location, style and budget, with verified local facts and disclosed featured placements, rather than a pay-to-rank ranking. City and category pages list only verified local vendors from the supplied data. Package prices and hours are not in the candidate data and must be checked on each vendor's own site before publishing.

## Rules
- Anonymous editorial-team byline. No named person, no personal story, no Person schema.
- Independent method; featured placements are always disclosed. No pay-to-rank.
- One intent per page; no doorway pages. City pages list only verified listings from the supplied data.
- Never invent a listing, a package, hours, prices, ratings, awards or reviews. If the data does not say it, leave it out.
- The data source is Overture Maps places (candidate list). Whether a place actually hosts weddings, plus hours and package prices, must still be checked on each business's own site before publishing, then dated.
- Flag any listing whose wedding-venue status looks doubtful (for example a hotel chain, a tattoo studio, a brewery whose name says it is closed, a board-game cafe, or a community resource centre tagged as an event venue).
- Nothing goes live without the owner's yes.

## Voice
Plain, factual, helpful. Short paragraphs. No hype.

## Banned phrases
- best in town
- world-famous
- hidden gem
- fairy-tale
- we personally visited
- our founder
